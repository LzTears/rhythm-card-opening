User requested pixel typography to match the selected workshop art references.

12px pixel font family with nearest filtering, no antialiasing or subpixel
positioning, and 24/36/48px size tiers. Long descriptions, settings explanations,
errors and tooltips retain Noto/MSDF for readability. Font binaries and licenses
are unchanged. Actual runtime sample and two-resolution verification are under
verification/workshop_migration; nominal font sizes alone are not pixel-grid QA.
